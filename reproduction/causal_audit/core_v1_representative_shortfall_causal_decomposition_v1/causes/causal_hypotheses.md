# Causal hypotheses F01-F11

All verdicts are pre-registered. A diagnostic result is never elevated above its evidence class.

## F01 environment_exposure

Hypothesis: Representative states may have insufficient physical, represented-map, dynamic, or downstream-gate exposure.

Allowed diagnostic: E1 reanalysis plus E5 asset audit

Prohibited inference: No physical-danger claim for behavior-only maps.

## F02 configuration_exposure

Hypothesis: Frozen velocity/dt/acceleration/latency regime may suppress activation.

Allowed diagnostic: E4 one-factor-at-a-time shadow regimes

Prohibited inference: No deployment or formal-performance claim.

## F03 b0_masking

Hypothesis: B0 current feasibility may left-truncate downstream B1/B2/B3 evaluation.

Allowed diagnostic: E1 funnel plus E2 independent-gate shadow

Prohibited inference: No shadow control is a committed action.

## F04 b1_timing

Hypothesis: Immediate position-first segment may be candidate-insensitive.

Allowed diagnostic: E2 S0-S3 same-dynamics shadow

Prohibited inference: S2/S3 are not a Core V1 replacement.

## F05 b2_atomicity

Hypothesis: Terminal/backup/braking composition may conceal component-level causes.

Allowed diagnostic: E2 atomic decomposition and L1-L5

Prohibited inference: Leave-one-out is not a new method.

## F06 b3_coverage

Hypothesis: Six slots may miss controls in a finite pre-registered search set.

Allowed diagnostic: E3 GRID7+SOBOL512 over C0/C1

Prohibited inference: No continuous control-space conclusion.

## F07 representative_distribution

Hypothesis: Frozen registries can be leakage-free yet not deployment/on-policy representative.

Allowed diagnostic: E5 asset inventory and read-only comparison

Prohibited inference: No initial-state-to-deployment inference.

## F08 metric_sensitivity

Hypothesis: Final action change can miss certificate or availability gains.

Allowed diagnostic: E1/E2/E3 metric taxonomy

Prohibited inference: Certificate gain is not behavioral gain.

## F09 map_representation

Hypothesis: Map type and reference authority can limit learned-map conclusions.

Allowed diagnostic: E5 representation/readiness audit

Prohibited inference: Proxy clearance is not physical clearance.

## F10 statistical_power

Hypothesis: Zero-event cohorts may not exclude low underlying event rates, especially downstream conditional rates.

Allowed diagnostic: E1 binomial/cluster/conditional analysis

Prohibited inference: Do not pool the 360 states.

## F11 implementation_consistency

Hypothesis: An adapter, serialization, or classification mismatch could explain zero results.

Allowed diagnostic: E0 hashes plus deterministic replays/formula checks

Prohibited inference: Any semantic mismatch forces Case G.
