# No Hidden Control Transformation Policy

The exact vector and identity that receive the final applicable certificate must be the vector and identity committed to the normative plant.

- A post-certification clip, saturation, frame transform, rate limiter, delay model, quantizer, or fallback is forbidden unless it is explicitly authorized before commitment.
- Any value-changing transform creates a new candidate identity and invalidates certificates attached to the source identity.
- The transformed candidate must re-enter actuator admission and every affected safety-certification stage.
- Queueing or logging metadata may not change the control.
- Missing actuator authority produces `UNKNOWN/BLOCK`, never a default or legacy fallback.
- `u_des` may equal the certified value numerically in a particular step, but it remains nominal until it passes the full authority chain.
