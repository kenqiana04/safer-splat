# D5: CERTIFICATE_AWARE_CONTINUOUS_CONTROL_OPTIMIZATION

- **Precise Problem**: Optimize minimum-intervention executable control continuously rather than enumerate a finite library.
- **Input Output**: Actuator-valid control or fail-closed result.
- **Novelty Claim**: A certificate-aware continuous optimizer jointly respects segment and terminal checks.
- **Target Community**: Optimization and control robotics
- **Top Three Competitors**: FOCI; SPLANNING; FastBridge
- **Exact Difference**: Optimizes the executable certificate rather than only Gaussian collision cost.
- **Minimum Method**: Differentiable/bilevel solver and fairness study.
- **Minimum Theory**: Feasibility and convergence bounds under limited assumptions.
- **Minimum Experiments**: Strong optimization baselines and closed-loop learned-map trials.
- **Required Datasets**: Two independent learned maps.
- **Required Reference**: Credible map references.
- **Required Hardware**: GPU workstation
- **Eight Week Deliverability**: low
- **Fallback Publication**: Optimization workshop fallback.
- **Fatal Risk**: FOCI/SPLANNING/FastBridge crowd the space; differentiating all certificate stages is high risk.
