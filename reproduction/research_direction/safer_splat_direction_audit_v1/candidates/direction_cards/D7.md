# D7: LEARNED_GAUSSIAN_MAP_NAVIGATION_QUALIFICATION_BENCHMARK

- **Precise Problem**: Measure whether learned Gaussian maps are navigation-qualified under reference-grounded false-free and UNKNOWN metrics.
- **Input Output**: Benchmark labels, splits, metrics, and baseline results.
- **Novelty Claim**: A cross-map navigation-qualification benchmark centered on one-sided false-free and abstention would fill a measurement gap.
- **Target Community**: Mapping, embodied vision, and robotics
- **Top Three Competitors**: Geometric accuracy evaluation; GAVIS; NavGSim
- **Exact Difference**: Connects map geometry error to navigation-query semantics and reference hierarchy.
- **Minimum Method**: Benchmark protocol, dataset adapters, and metrics.
- **Minimum Theory**: Statistical reliability and split rationale, not a control theorem.
- **Minimum Experiments**: At least two learned maps with references and sufficient events.
- **Required Datasets**: Two or more independent map families/scenes.
- **Required Reference**: Mesh/depth/laser authority.
- **Required Hardware**: GPU workstation and storage
- **Eight Week Deliverability**: medium
- **Fallback Publication**: Benchmark/measurement venue.
- **Fatal Risk**: Current assets do not yet satisfy two independent learned maps with credible reference and event labels.
