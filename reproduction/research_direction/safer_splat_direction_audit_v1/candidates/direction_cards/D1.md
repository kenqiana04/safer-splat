- **Precise Problem**: Use Core V1 only as a rare-event executable supervisor.
- **Input Output**: Typed commit/fail-closed decision for rare states.
- **Novelty Claim**: A rare-event positioning of actuator/swept/backup/directional checks improves intervention semantics beyond a current-state CBF.
- **Target Community**: Safety-filter and field-robotics community
- **Top Three Competitors**: FastBridge; Predicted-Flow CBF; SAFER-Splat
- **Exact Difference**: Restricts scope to rare events and typed abstention rather than broad replacement.
- **Minimum Method**: A calibrated rare-event trigger and supervisor.
- **Minimum Theory**: Sampled-data terminal/backup obligation.
- **Minimum Experiments**: Two learned maps with nonzero trigger events and three baselines.
- **Required Datasets**: Two independent learned maps.
- **Required Reference**: Credible geometry on both maps.
- **Required Hardware**: Optional robot; GPU workstation
- **Eight Week Deliverability**: low-medium
- **Fallback Publication**: Mechanism note or negative prevalence study.
- **Fatal Risk**: FastBridge already couples actuator-aware 3DGS filtering and backup CBF; representative events are zero.
